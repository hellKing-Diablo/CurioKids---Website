About CurioKids

Nurturing curiosity and confidence in kids through experiences that feel like play.

CurioKids is a private learning and engagement organization focused on workshops, events, and birthday experiences for schools and children. We design each activity to be interactive, age-appropriate, and rooted in life-skill development.

Our programs combine creativity, communication, teamwork, and hands-on exploration. Whether we are running a school workshop or hosting a birthday event, the goal stays the same: meaningful learning with joyful participation.

Our Mission

To create memorable learning moments that help kids discover skills, confidence, and curiosity.

Our Focus

Workshops, events, and curated birthday formats built specifically for school-age learners.
